# Code of conduct

Tempora is a game about many peoples across many centuries, and its community should welcome people from all of them.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagree with the idea itself, and explain why.
- Respect that people play, write and contribute differently.
- Accept feedback on your work gracefully, and give it constructively.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about any group of people.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised content or unwanted attention.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and anywhere you represent the project, including the in-game community on servers run by the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Mofferato), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on any comment. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code and issues that break these rules, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who repeatedly break them.
